What it does

Every incoming delivery is logged as a batch with its own expiry date. When stock goes out — over the counter or through a bulk order — Sellwell picks the batch expiring soonest automatically (First-Expiry-First-Out), and simply refuses to touch anything already expired. You always know exactly how much of a medicine is actually sellable, right now.

FEFO dispensing

Stock always leaves in expiry order — earliest batch first — computed automatically, never left to memory or a sticky note on the shelf.

In-date stock, instantly

"Do we have paracetamol in date?" is a one-second lookup, not a walk to the back shelf to check dates by hand.

Expiry alerts

A running list of batches approaching their expiry date, so you can discount, return, or reorder before stock turns to a write-off.

Search, sort & filter

Find any medicine or batch in seconds and sort your inventory by stock level, expiry date, or price.

Who it's for

Independent pharmacists

Running the counter and the stockroom yourself — you need the FEFO decision made for you, not another spreadsheet to maintain.

Pharmacy chains & staff

Multiple staff dispensing from the same shelves need one shared, always-current source of truth for what's safe to sell.

How it helps

Expired stock is a double loss — money already spent, and a compliance risk if it ever reaches a customer. By enforcing FEFO at the point of dispensing and surfacing expiry alerts early, Sellwell turns "check the dates" from a manual habit into a rule the system never forgets.
